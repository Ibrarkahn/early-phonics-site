Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Early Phonics is a children's educational app published by Komfany. We take privacy seriously, particularly because the app is designed for young children.

Information we collect

Early Phonics does not require users to create an account and does not ask children to provide their name, email address, phone number or other directly identifying personal information.

The app may collect limited technical and usage information through services such as Firebase Analytics. This may include information such as app interactions, device type, app version and general usage events.

Advertising

Early Phonics may display advertising using Google AdMob. Where advertising is shown, the app is configured with child-directed and age-appropriate settings intended for apps used by children.

Personalised advertising and interest-based advertising are not intended to be used for child users.

How information is used

Usage information may be used to understand how the app is used, improve reliability, improve learning features and identify technical issues.

Third-party services

Early Phonics may use third-party services including Google Firebase Analytics and Google AdMob. These providers may process limited technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable child-safety requirements.

Children's privacy

Early Phonics is designed for children and is intended to comply with applicable Google Play Families requirements. We aim to minimise data collection and avoid requesting personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the app, legal requirements or third-party services.
